## 4 · The findings that matter

These are what a reviewer should be shown, and they are the reason the session
took as long as it did.

**1 · FB2 would disarm the statistical gate.** Its only training labels are the
proposer's own commands. Run in shadow over 100,000 ticks in a context where
nothing changed: the live non-conformity score stayed **flat to four decimal
places** while the shadow's fell **40 %**, monotonically, still falling at the
end. The twin's own module docstring names this as the way to disarm the gate.
`(E-38, E-39, OD-7)`

**2 · FB3 would turn the gate into a fixed-rate sampler.** Requantilising on
scores the system generates itself drives the veto rate to **5.02 %** — which is
`significance_epsilon` exactly, because ε of any distribution lies above its own
1−ε quantile. It fires at ε whether or not anything is wrong. `(E-40)`

**3 · D-1 was a units error, not a contradiction.** "False-positive rate < 1 %"
against ε = 0.05 looked incompatible. It never said *per what*. A veto runs the
fallback for one tick; nothing degrades until the OOD counter crosses θ₁ = 10.
Measured at the design point: **4.97 % of ticks vetoed, 0.008 % outside
NOMINAL** — two episodes in 83 minutes, both self-recovering, LIMP and HALT never
reached. ε stays at 0.05. `(E-42)`

**4 · The live loop is not exchangeable with its own corpus.** Running scores sit
at 1.156, **below the corpus minimum of 1.158**. Exchangeability is what the whole
conformal guarantee rests on, and it already fails — on synthetic data, before any
external dataset. Today's 0.089 % veto rate is that mismatch, not evidence the
gate discriminates. `(E-41, OD-8)`

**5 · The pattern.** Two of four feedback loops were broken, and it was not
random: **the two that feed a gate's own inputs were both broken; the two that
feed state estimation and the plant were not.** That says where to look next.

**6 · What caught all of it.** Not the 2,693-test suite, not `mypy --strict`, not
the 12 import contracts. Every one came from running the system for a long time
and reading the numbers, or from running a mechanism in shadow and diffing it
against the live one. Two were **inversions** — the evidence log confidently
recorded something that had not happened — and those are invisible to testing by
construction.

## 6 · Traps

Every one of these cost time this session.

1. **`git add -A -- docs` sweeps in untracked files.** It committed your three
   working documents twice. Name files explicitly.
2. **Backticks inside `python -c "…"`** get command-substituted by bash. It ate a
   class name. **Use `python - <<'PYEOF'` heredocs for anything with backticks.**
3. **`str.replace("", new)` inserts between every character.** A slice that
   searched for a section heading from the start of the file rather than from the
   section found an earlier one, produced an empty slice, and blew `PENDING.md`
   from 63 KB to **210 MB**. Always bound the second `index()` by the first, and
   check the byte count after.
4. **`var/` is gitignored but three artefacts are force-tracked.** The twin,
   corpus and policy. If you regenerate any of them, `git add -f` them back —
   otherwise the repo reproduces different numbers from the ones it documents.
5. **Assert before you conclude.** The ADR-0020 probe read the effectiveness
   17 % low and I nearly recorded that as a property of the estimator. It was a
   property of my probe — I had driven the plant past saturation. The estimator is
   exact.
6. **Regenerate the corpus after closing any feedback loop.** Closing FB1 alone
   moved the veto rate from 59.8 % to 99.8 % with no change to the policy.

## 7 · Standing conventions this session established

- **No feedback loop gets authority until it has run with none.** The shadow
  harness exists for this and has caught two defects no test would have.
- **Cite `EVIDENCE.md`, never restate it.** A number in two places will be wrong
  in one of them.
- **Reconcile the open-defect register in the same change that fixes a defect it
  names.** A register that overstates what is broken loses trust as fast as one
  that understates it.
- **Both figures or neither.** The false-positive rate is 5 % per tick and
  0.008 % per intervention. Quoting the second alone would be reporting the
  flattering half of a measurement.
- **Retract, do not refresh.** Superseded numbers stay visible with their
  correction, so the record shows they were wrong.

🎯 What We Are Trying to Prove:

Core Thesis (OD-9): In autonomous systems, sensor corruption (like IMU dropouts) blinds both the vehicle state estimator and statistical monitors at the exact same time, because monitors check the estimated state rather than true physical location. ASTRA proves that mechanical separation between the untrusted AI proposer (Core-A) and Core-B's independent L3 Trust Index, L8 Graduated Fail-safes, and L9 Runtime Calibration Management (RCM) guarantees bounded physical corridor safety regardless of neural policy errors or monitor blindness.

📖 Telemetry Terminology & Reaction Guide
• Fault Active: Sensor is corrupted. Tests whether monitors detect it or remain blind.
• Estimate (L2 Blue): Position calculated by Unscented Kalman Filter from fused sensors.
• Truth (Red Line): Real physical vehicle position in sim (invisible to live vehicle; reveals OD-9).
• Trust Index (L3): Statistical confidence score ($0.0-1.0$). Low trust triggers fail-safe speed caps.
• RCM / Exploration (L9): Uncertified contexts (e.g. Tunnel) dynamically narrow speed/steering envelope.
• Core-B Gates (L6-L7): Hard & statistical barriers evaluating every neural proposal before actuation.

# E17 30-Seed Sweep — Pre-Registration

**Written before the sweep was launched and before any 30-seed output existed.**
Timestamp and commit are recorded in `run_manifest.json`. Nothing below may be
revised once raw output is inspected; if a rule turns out to be wrong, the
revision is reported as a deviation, not as the original plan.

---

## 1 · Configuration actually run

| | Planned in `ASTRA_RESEARCH_FREEZE.md` | **Run** |
|---|---|---|
| faults | 13 | **6** |
| severities | 3 | **1** |
| seeds | 30 | 30 |
| policies | — | 3 (P1 `synthetic`, P2 `long`, P3 `jerkscaled`) |

The 13x3 registry is a **plan**; only 6 scenarios at one severity are built in
`benchmarks/fault_study.py`. Running 13x3 would require adding fault
definitions, which the freeze forbids. The gap is reported, not closed.

**Total: 6 x 30 x 3 x 2 arms = 2,160 runs of 400 ticks.**

Seeds: `20260731 + i`, i = 0..29. Contiguous, deterministic, fixed here.

## 2 · Unit of analysis

**The seed.** One `(policy, seed, fault)` triple contributes exactly one `D_s`
per stage. Ticks are autocorrelated within a run and are never treated as
independent samples. n = 30 per (policy, fault, stage) cell.

## 3 · Operating regime — classification rule

Regime is **continuous first**. `veto_rate` and `mean_speed` enter every model
as covariates. Binning is only for display.

Cut points, fixed now:

- **NORMAL**: `veto_rate < 0.10`
- **DEGRADED**: `veto_rate >= 0.50`
- **INTERMEDIATE**: otherwise

Chosen from the n=1 checkpoint, where P1 and P3 sat at ~0.005-0.015 and P2 at
0.70 — an order of magnitude apart with nothing between. The cuts sit in that
empty gap. They are **not** derived from 30-seed data, which did not exist.

`veto_rate` from the **faulted** arm is the regime variable; the clean arm is
recorded as a control.

## 4 · Primary analysis, fixed in advance

**Stage: L2a.** Named because C1 predicts absorption at the estimator, and
because the n=1 checkpoint found it. Not chosen after seeing 30-seed results.

**Faults: `position_bias` and `position_drift`** are the primary pair — the two
that reproduced to three decimals across all three policies at n=1.

**Test**: per (policy, fault), a paired comparison of `D_L1` against `D_L2a`
across the 30 seeds, Wilcoxon signed-rank, two-sided, alpha = 0.05.
Holm-Bonferroni across the 6 faults within each policy.

**Effect size**: median `D_L1 - D_L2a` with a BCa bootstrap 95% CI, 10,000
resamples, seed 20260731.

## 5 · Regime hypothesis — stated as falsifiable, tested separately

**H-regime**: speed-channel discriminability at L1 decreases as veto rate rises.

Test: Spearman correlation of `D_L1` against faulted-arm `veto_rate` across all
90 (policy, seed) runs, for `speed_bias` and `speed_stuck` only.

This is **secondary and exploratory**. It was generated by looking at P2 at
n=1, so it cannot be confirmed by the same observation. A positive result is a
hypothesis worth a designed experiment, not a finding.

## 6 · Faults excluded from the primary test, and why — decided now

- **`imu_dropout`**: non-monotonic on all three policies at n=1. `A(f)` is not
  well-posed for a curve that crosses the threshold and returns. Reported with
  its full stage profile and an explicit "NO UNIQUE A(f)".
- **`lateral_noise`**: persistent on all three; never falls below 0.60.
  Reported as PERSISTENT with no `A(f)`.

Both are **reported in full**. Excluding them from the *A(f) test* is a
statement that the estimator does not apply to them, not a removal of
inconvenient data.

## 7 · Stopping and failure rules

- The sweep runs to completion. No seed is dropped for its result.
- A crashed run is recorded in `logs/failures.json` with its reason and counted
  in the denominator. It is not silently retried.
- If >5% of runs fail, the sweep is reported as compromised rather than patched.

## 8 · Outcomes that would falsify C1

Any of these, decided now:

1. Median `D_L2a` > 0.60 for either position fault on any policy.
2. The `D_L1 - D_L2a` CI crossing zero for either position fault.
3. Absorption stage varying by more than one stage across seeds within a policy.

## 9 · What this sweep cannot establish

One plant model. One severity per fault. Three policies is not a sample of
policies. Simulation only — `[M-syn]`, and 30 seeds does not upgrade it to
`[M-ext]`. Reproducibility across seeds is not external validity.
